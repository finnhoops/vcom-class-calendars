Ask 3 questions to find your row. The plan is the same for adults and kids; only the doses change.

1. Is there a pulse?No → rows 1-2 (arrest) · Yes →2. Too slow or too fast?→3. Unstable?(low BP, confused, shock, chest pain, heart failure)
No pulse = CPR + epiEvery arrest gets CPR and epinephrine every 3-5 min. Shock only VF/VT.
Unstable = electricityPulse but unstable: shock it with synchronized cardioversion (too fast) or pace it (too slow).
Stable = drugsPulse and stable: you have time for vagal maneuvers and drugs.

Kid dose shortcut: "point-oh-one, 2-4, 5, point-one, point-oh-two"

  • Epi 0.01 mg/kg (= 0.1 mL/kg)
  • Shock 2 then 4 J/kg
  • Amio 5 mg/kg
  • Adenosine 0.1 then 0.2 mg/kg
  • Atropine 0.02 mg/kg

Adult dose shortcut: "1, 300-150, 6-12, 1 to 3"

  • Epi 1 mg every 3-5 min
  • Amio 300 then 150 mg
  • Adenosine 6 then 12 mg
  • Atropine 1 mg, max 3 mg
  • CPR: adults 30:2, kids 15:2 with 2 rescuers
