H's: hypovolemia, hypoxia, H+ (acidosis), hypo/hyperkalemia, hypothermia
T's: tension pneumo, tamponade, toxins, thrombosis (PE), thrombosis (MI)
Pulse back (ROSC)? You'll see a pulse/BP or a sudden jump in ETCO2. → Post-arrest goals: SpO2 90-98%, PaCO2 35-45, MAP 65 or more, get a 12-lead. Not following commands → temperature control 32-37.5 °C for at least 36 h.
Don't shock asystole or PEA. Don't give amiodarone for them either.
Don't give atropine or vasopressin in an arrest. Don't stop CPR for more than 10 seconds.

3. PALS: child in cardiac arrest (no pulse)

Start CPR now. 100-120/min, 1/3 of the chest depth. 15:2 with 2 rescuers, 30:2 with 1. Give O2 (kids usually arrest from breathing problems). Pads/monitor on.
Is the rhythm shockable?
Yes: VF or pulseless VT
SHOCK 2 J/kg
CPR 2 min. Get an IV or IO.
still VF/VT
SHOCK 4 J/kg
CPR 2 min + Epinephrine 0.01 mg/kg(0.1 mL/kg), max 1 mg, every 3-5 min
still VF/VT
SHOCK 4 J/kg or more (max 10 J/kg)
CPR 2 min + Amiodarone 5 mg/kgcan repeat up to 3 doses
Keep looping. Treat the H's and T's + hypoglycemia.
No: asystole or PEA

CPR 2 min. Get IV/IO.

no

Don't use adult doses. Everything is per kg (epi max 1 mg).
